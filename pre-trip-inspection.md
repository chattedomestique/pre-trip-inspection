## *BRAKE SYSTEM CHECK*

**TELL THE TESTER YOU ARE NOW GOING TO CHECK THE ENTIRE BRAKE SYSTEM ON THE BUS.**

1. With Parking Brake **Engaged** – make sure you have between **120-140 PSI**. Then put the bus in Drive – accelerate to **1100 RPMs**.
   a. This will **check** the parking brake – **SAY: "The parking brake holds properly."**
2. Apply **RIGHT** foot to the **brake pedal** (Make sure you have between **120-140 PSI**)
   a. **Disengage** parking brake
   b. **Put** the bus in gear
   c. **Take** your **foot off** the brake pedal, allowing the **bus** to roll **SLIGHTLY** forward
   d. **Press** on the **brake pedal** to stop the bus. **Put the bus back in Neutral, re-apply brake**
   e. **Tell Tester – "The service brake works properly, there is no pulling from one side to the other."**
3. **Tell the tester – "I'm allowing the pressure to build back up to normal range of 120/140 psi."**
4. Once **pressure** has **reached** the **cut-off point**, **you must say – "I am now testing that the air brake system functions properly."**
5. **Shut down** the bus and then **turn** the **key to the right** – This turns the **power back on** to the bus
6. **YOU MUST SAY: "I am now going to test that the air in both tanks does not drop more than 3 lbs. of pressure in one minute. Would you time me?"**
   a. **Press foot on THE SERVICE BRAKE** then **DISENGAGE** the **parking brake** and **hold** your **foot** on the **service brake** for **one minute**. (It actually will not be one minute)
   b. You will see **both air tank needles drop** in pressure, then stop. That's when you indicate to the tester: **"There was an initial drop in pressure but both tanks are holding."**
7. When the **tester tells you** to continue: **You will then begin to pump your brake fast**
8. **You must indicate and say: The lights and buzzers will sound at 55 psi and at 40 psi the parking brake reengages (pops out).** *Specify exact values per bus driven.*
9. **YOU MUST SAY: "The air brake system is functioning properly."**
10. **NOW TELL THE TESTER YOU ARE STARTING THE BUS TO BUILD AIR PRESSURE BACK UP IN BOTH TANKS TO READ 120-140**

***IF YOU DO NOT COMPLETE ALL THE STEPS OF THE AIR BRAKE CHECK, IT IS AN AUTOMATIC FAIL OF THE TEST***

## *HELPFUL HINTS TO REMEMBER*

1. Slow your roll for more control, watch your speed!!!
2. DON'T FORGET TO LOOK / TURN YOUR HEAD LEFT AND RIGHT BEFORE, DURING, AND AFTER AS YOU GO THROUGH INTERSECTIONS!!!!
3. USE YOUR BLINKERS
4. HOLD YOUR LANE
5. DON'T CROSS OVER THE WHITE LINE AT INTERSECTIONS
6. LEAVE YOURSELF AN OUT WHEN STOPPING BEHIND SOMEONE
7. KEEP YOUR EYES MOVING AND CHECK YOUR MIRRORS
8. Stop behind the white line at stop sign, then move up to see and come to a full stop again and bob and weave before pulling out.
9. KEEP BOTH HANDS ON STEERING WHEEL / NO PALMING or talking with your hands
10. WHEN MAKING LEFT-HAND TURNS DO NOT CREEP OUT INTO THE INTERSECTION
11. ALWAYS CHECK MIRRORS FOR DUAL PLACEMENT WHEN TURNING AND WATCH YOUR TAIL SWING
12. TRY TO AVOID BACKING UP, IT'S VERY DANGEROUS!!! But if you have to back up, start in proper position for the type of backing you need to do, check your surroundings, back slowly, and back towards the driver side because it's easier to see. If someone is there to guide you, ask them to help you.
